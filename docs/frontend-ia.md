# Sori frontend information architecture

The Phase 1 product navigation is intentionally small and utility-oriented:

1. Home
2. Transcripts
3. Vocabulary
4. Voice Edit
5. Models & Routing
6. Benchmarks
7. Extensions
8. Privacy
9. Diagnostics
10. Settings

Prototype-only screens (such as onboarding and Labs) are not product navigation items. MVP settings do not expose team or billing controls. Vocabulary is the user-facing name for the personal dictionary, and Extensions is the user-facing name for integrations.

Home copy uses **Teach Sori your words**. The desktop title bar shows **Route: Local · Whisper Q5**.
